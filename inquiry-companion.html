Discovery Companion

Three gentle learning adventures for Kindergarten. No scores or student records are collected here; existing lessons and assessments remain the source of progress.

1 · Notice, compare, explain

Gather six safe everyday objects. Sort them by color or shape; then invite the learner to invent a different sorting rule. Ask: “How do you know these belong together?” Offer pointing, drawing, or verbal answers.

2 · Make a tiny story

Choose three pictures or familiar toys. Arrange a beginning, a problem and a helpful ending. Ask the learner to tell the story aloud or draw what happens next. Practice one new descriptive word without requiring perfect spelling.

3 · Numbers in everyday life

Use two groups of counters with up to ten objects each. Count slowly, compare more, fewer or equal, and explain the difference by pairing objects one to one. Invite a second strategy.

Family and mentor notes

Observe curiosity, reasoning and kindness. Give extra processing time and offer accessible alternatives; avoid ranking children by speed. For a bridge into Grade 1, ask the learner to record one observation through a picture and a short sentence.
