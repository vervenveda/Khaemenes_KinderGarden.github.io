# Kindergarten Social Studies · Florida State Pack

Use very simple Florida examples at required localization checkpoints while preserving the national Kindergarten course and 80% mastery progression.

Examples may include a Florida map shape, Gulf/Atlantic water, local parks or public places, community helpers, state/local symbols and landmarks, museums/library pictures, present-day Seminole and Miccosukee community sources where appropriate, weather/environment and shared-space care, and simple then-and-now local photographs.

The learner may point, draw, match, tell, or dictate. Record the observed evidence in the localization-evidence field. No private family information is required.
