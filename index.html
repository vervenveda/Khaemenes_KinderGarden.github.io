Kinder Garden

A deliberate play on words — a kindergarten designed as a real garden for growing minds.

Kinder Garden now brings together a complete 36-week kindergarten curriculum with 180 daily lessons, printable packets, weekly assessments, family-controlled records, local learning apps, and the strongest age-appropriate adventures from Crechè. Children can learn formally, practice through play, create, move, wonder, and grow without losing the gentleness of the early-learning years.

Mentor communication style
Review Parent / Guardian Release & Authorization

Educational role. Khaemenes Academy, Crechè Preschool, Kinder Garden, the Khaemenes Mentor, and related learning pages are educational and informational tools. They are not accredited-school, childcare, medical, therapy, emergency, or professional-service substitutes.

Adult responsibility. The parent or guardian remains responsible for supervision, safety, healthcare, internet/device use, educational decisions, and deciding whether activities are appropriate.

AI Mentor boundaries. The Mentor may organize activities, encourage, summarize structured learning records, provide age-aligned prompts, and recommend breaks or adult help. It is not a human teacher, guardian, counselor, clinician, or decision-maker. Child-facing Kinder Garden does not provide unrestricted private chat.

Custom avatars. A custom visible mentor changes presentation only; it does not change safety rules, age alignment, or adult responsibilities.

Learning records. The Mentor may use nickname, broad age band, interests, mentor style, favorites, visits, structured feedback, and local progress to personalize learning. These are educational observations, not diagnoses.

Respectful use. Do not deliberately abuse, exploit, manipulate, or attempt to bypass Mentor safeguards or introduce age-inappropriate material.

Privacy. The current learner profile is local-first and avoids surname, exact birthday, email, address, photograph, student ID, or required cloud account. Families retain local review/export/erase control.

No guaranteed outcome. Learning tools may support practice, curiosity, routine, and exploration but do not promise a particular academic, developmental, therapeutic, or future outcome.
