Kinder Garden

Letters · Numbers · Nature · Stories · Art · Music · Friendship · Wonder

At Khaemenes Academy, Kinder Garden is written as two words on purpose. It is a garden where children are nurtured with kindness, imagination, patience, creativity, discovery, and room to grow at their own pace.
