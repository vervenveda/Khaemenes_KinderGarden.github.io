Teacher & Family Learning Notes

Week 32 · Five distinct learning experiences

This week develops Aquatic habitats, water safety, animals, maps, and environmental care. The essential question is “How is water a home?” Children may respond through speech, pointing, AAC, movement, drawing, building, signing, selecting, or adult dictation. Support is documented separately from mastery.

Across the week

Literacy: Water habitat vocabulary

Mathematics: Sort/count water animals

Inquiry: Habitats and resources

SEL: Care and safety near water

Maker / expression: Water habitat mural

Monday · Wonder Launch

Purpose: Launch the week by noticing what children already know about Aquatic habitats, water safety, animals, maps, and environmental care, naming the essential question, and building shared vocabulary through story, movement, objects, and conversation.

Guided practice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Teacher-led picture/object sort plus a short manipulative demonstration connecting Water habitat vocabulary and Sort/count water animals.

Independent choice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Children choose one center to draw, build, sort, match, act, or tell what they noticed.

Reflection: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Child completes: “I noticed ___.” Response may be spoken, signed, pointed, drawn, dictated, or selected.

Evidence note: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Observe entry vocabulary, safe participation, one literacy behavior, and one mathematical/inquiry behavior. Record evidence without turning participation into mastery.

Tuesday · Guided Skill Lab

Purpose: Practice the week’s literacy and mathematics skills for Aquatic habitats, water safety, animals, maps, and environmental care with explicit modeling, manipulatives, oral rehearsal, and small-group feedback.

Guided practice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Small-group literacy/math lab with teacher prompts, manipulatives, picture cards, and repeated practice.

Independent choice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: A choice board offers one literacy task, one math task, and one combined play task.

Reflection: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Child shows or tells one strategy that worked and one part they want to practice again.

Evidence note: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Collect a brief skill snapshot from the guided lab; mark support level and strategy used, not just correct/incorrect.

Wednesday · Inquiry Workshop

Purpose: Investigate Habitats and resources by observing, predicting, testing, sorting, mapping, comparing, or asking questions while using the week’s language and math.

Guided practice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Teacher-supported inquiry station using safe materials, a prediction, an observation, and a simple evidence record.

Independent choice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Children repeat or extend the investigation at a center, then place one observation in the class evidence display.

Reflection: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Child completes: “I thought ___. I noticed ___. Now I think ___.” with any accessible communication mode.

Evidence note: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Look for safe inquiry behavior, a real observation, and an evidence-based statement. Adult prompting is documented separately.

Thursday · Create & Communicate

Purpose: Create, build, move, draw, sing, dramatize, or present a product that communicates understanding of Aquatic habitats, water safety, animals, maps, and environmental care and connects to the maker project: Water habitat mural.

Guided practice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Teacher conference or maker mini-lesson focused on planning, material use, and communicating an idea clearly.

Independent choice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Children work on Water habitat mural using art, construction, movement, dramatic play, music, or mixed media.

Reflection: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Child identifies one choice they made and what they wanted another person to notice or understand.

Evidence note: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Document planning, persistence, communication, and connection to the week’s idea. Artistic neatness is not a mastery criterion.

Friday · Review, Assessment & Reflection

Purpose: Retrieve, apply, and reflect on the week’s learning about Aquatic habitats, water safety, animals, maps, and environmental care; complete formal evidence without confusing confidence, speed, speech volume, or compliance with mastery.

Guided practice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Brief review stations target misconceptions found during the week before the formal demonstration.

Independent choice: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Child completes the week’s demonstration and selects a portfolio artifact or reflection response.

Reflection: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Child answers one growth prompt: “I can…,” “I learned…,” or “I want to practice…”

Evidence note: Week 32 — Water Worlds: Pond, River, and Ocean: Use the published weekly rubric. Formal progression requires at least 80% plus any stated essential criteria; participation evidence alone never awards mastery.

Mastery safeguard

Daily participation and work samples show the learning journey. They do not automatically award mastery. Formal advancement continues to require the published weekly rubric at 80% or higher, including any essential criteria named for the week.
