# Khaemenes Academy Social Studies · State Localization Policy

Every Khaemenes Academy Social Studies grade is state-localizable.

The program uses a **national-core / state-local-overlay** model. State/local maps, public institutions, symbols, historical sources, biographies, Indigenous histories, environments, economic examples, and civic cases should be used where appropriate, while the national disciplinary core and Academy 80% mastery policy remain intact.

Localization must be evidence-based, nonpartisan in civic/election work, protective of learner privacy, and respectful of specific Indigenous nations and present-day continuity.

See `social-studies/STATE_LOCALIZATION_GUIDE.md` for Kindergarten implementation.
