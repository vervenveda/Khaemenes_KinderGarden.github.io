Teacher Tools

Organized support for every kindergarten week.

Everything here is local, printable, and designed to support a developmentally appropriate A+ kindergarten program without adding outside curriculum dependencies.

01

Pacing Guide

Use one unit per week and five lessons per unit, with flexible center time, review, enrichment, and reteach opportunities as needed.

02

Observation Checklist

Track oral language, early literacy, mathematics, inquiry, social-emotional growth, movement, creativity, and classroom participation.

03

Portfolio Rubric

Collect at least one meaningful weekly sample such as a drawing, dictated story, mathematics task, inquiry record, or project observation note.

04

Materials Planner

Prepare counters, blocks, picture cards, paper, crayons, story materials, movement space, manipulatives, and a calm learning area.

05

Intervention Map

When a learner is not ready, reteach through play, movement, smaller steps, visuals, modeling, and oral response before increasing pencil-based work.

06

Family Communication

Send concise weekly notes with one guiding question, key vocabulary, a home observation, a read-or-tell prompt, and something worth celebrating.

Daily Practice & Documentation

Teacher Flow & Weekly Evidence

A simple daily rhythm for consistent instruction, observation, and family-controlled documentation.

Daily Teacher Flow

  1. Welcome the learner, review the visual schedule, and complete a brief feelings check-in.
  2. Read, tell, sing, or introduce the unit idea in child-friendly language.
  3. Model the target skill using objects, pictures, movement, demonstration, and conversation.
  4. Practice the skill through small groups, guided work, or learning centers.
  5. Create, build, draw, move, investigate, or dramatize the concept.
  6. Collect one observation note, response, photograph note, or work sample as evidence.
  7. Close with learner reflection using a prompt such as “I learned…” or “I noticed…”
