Teacher Tools

Kindergarten Mentor Toolkit

Everything here is local, printable, and designed for a developmentally appropriate A+ kindergarten program.

01

Pacing Guide

Use one unit per week, five lessons per unit, with flexible center time and review/reteach days.

02

Observation Checklist

Track oral language, early literacy, math, inquiry, SEL, movement, creativity, and participation.

03

Portfolio Rubric

Collect one weekly sample: drawing, dictated story, math task, inquiry evidence, or project photo note.

04

Materials Planner

Prepare counters, blocks, picture cards, paper, crayons, story basket, movement space, and calm corner.

05

Intervention Map

If a child is not ready, reteach through play, movement, smaller steps, visuals, and oral response before pencil tasks.

06

Family Communication

Send short weekly notes: question, vocabulary, home observation, read/tell prompt, and celebration.

Daily Teacher Flow

  1. Welcome, visual schedule, feelings check-in.
  2. Read/tell/sing the unit idea.
  3. Model the skill with objects, pictures, movement, and language.
  4. Practice in small groups or centers.
  5. Create, build, draw, move, or dramatize.
  6. Collect one evidence note or work sample.
  7. Close with child reflection: “I learned…” or “I noticed…”
