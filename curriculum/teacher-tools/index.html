Teacher & Family Tools

Kindergarten Mentor Toolkit

Local, printable guidance for a developmentally appropriate 36-week kindergarten program.

01

Pacing Guide

Use one integrated unit per week, five lessons per unit, with flexible center time, review, and reteaching.

02

Observation Checklist

Track oral language, early literacy, mathematics, inquiry, SEL, movement, creativity, and participation.

03

Portfolio Evidence

Collect a weekly sample such as drawing, dictated story, math task, inquiry evidence, project, or adult observation note.

04

Materials Planner

Prepare counters, blocks, picture cards, paper, crayons, story materials, movement space, and a calm corner.

05

Reteach Map

When a learner needs another pathway, use play, movement, smaller steps, visuals, modeling, oral response, and additional practice before adding pencil demands.

06

Family Communication

Share the week's essential question, vocabulary, home observation, read/tell prompt, practice companion, and celebration.

Daily Teacher Flow

  1. Welcome, visual schedule, and feelings check-in.
  2. Read, tell, sing, or demonstrate the unit idea.
  3. Model the skill with objects, pictures, movement, and language.
  4. Practice in a small group or learning center.
  5. Create, build, draw, move, investigate, or dramatize.
  6. Use the matched Kinder Garden / Crechè companion when it supports today's objective.
  7. Collect one evidence note or work sample.
  8. Close with child reflection: “I learned…” or “I noticed…”
