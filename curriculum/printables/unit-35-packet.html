Kinder Garden · Week 35

Independent Inquiry and Investigation

Practice packet · Complete using words, drawings, counters or another accessible communication method. Practice does not award formal mastery.

Day 1 · Ask a testable question

Try it: Draw two sets of leaves or paper shapes. Write or dictate one question you can answer by observing or counting.

Draw, sort, write, or attach evidence here

Explain: I wonder __________. I already know __________.

Adult observation / feedback: ______________________________________________

Day 2 · Plan your investigation

Try it: Choose counters, a sorting mat, or equal-size units; draw first/next/last steps.

Draw, sort, write, or attach evidence here

Explain: My tool is __________ because __________.

Adult observation / feedback: ______________________________________________

Day 3 · Record observations

Try it: Draw two seed trays with 3 and 5 seedlings. Record two facts and a separate prediction.

Draw, sort, write, or attach evidence here

Explain: I notice __________. I think __________.

Adult observation / feedback: ______________________________________________

Day 4 · Make a model or graph

Try it: Make a labeled graph for 4 circles and 2 triangles; verify each represented count.

Draw, sort, write, or attach evidence here

Explain: The evidence shows __________.

Adult observation / feedback: ______________________________________________

Day 5 · Explain and reflect

Try it: Use a new group of 3 and 5 picture cards. State the question, method, two observations and a next question.

Draw, sort, write, or attach evidence here

Explain: My next question is __________.
