Monday · Discover — Who Belongs Here?

Objective: Children identify themselves as members of a learning community and begin using names, classroom language, and routines to communicate belonging.

🎵 Morning Circle

Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and the question: “What helps people feel welcome and ready to learn?”

📚 Literacy

Find and recognize their own name; notice that spoken names can be represented with print.

🔢 Math

Count a small collection of classroom objects and match one object to one counting word.

🔬 Inquiry

Walk through the classroom and notice places, materials, people, and routines.

💛 SEL

Name a feeling or need and practice a help-seeking sentence.

🎨 Workshop

Build a shared “Learning Garden” anchor chart. Children create a first name/self-portrait artifact.

🧸 Centers

Name/print table, math bin, art station, dramatic play, movement/music, book corner, calm spot.

📋 Teacher Evidence

Can the child identify their name, communicate a need, count a small collection, and describe one classroom feature?

🌈 How Do You Feel Today?

Choose one feeling. Pointing, tapping, saying the word, or choosing with a grown-up all count.

🔢 Count With Me

Tap each classroom object once.

0 objects counted

Tuesday · Investigate — How Do We Know Where Things Belong?

Objective: Children investigate how classroom materials are organized and use attributes to sort and classify objects.

🎵 Morning Circle

Revisit Monday observations; introduce “same,” “different,” “belongs,” and “does not belong.”

📚 Literacy

Match name cards to children and classroom labels; listen for beginning sounds in familiar names.

🔢 Math

Sort classroom objects by one attribute, then explain the sorting rule.

🔬 Inquiry

Investigate why materials have designated places and how organization helps a community work.

💛 SEL

Practice asking before taking a shared material and returning materials safely.

🎨 Workshop

Small groups sort a mixed collection, draw the groups, and explain the rule used.

🧸 Centers

Label-and-match station, sorting lab, construction station, dramatic play, movement/music, book corner, calm spot.

📋 Teacher Evidence

Can the child sort by an observable attribute and explain the rule using words, gesture, drawing, or demonstration?

🧺 Sorting Game

Tap an object, then tap where it belongs.

Choose an object to begin.

Wednesday · Reason — What Makes a Classroom Work?

Objective: Children connect routines and responsibilities to classroom safety, learning, and belonging.

🎵 Morning Circle

Examine classroom scenarios. Ask, “What happened? What could we change?”

📚 Literacy

Sequence picture cards showing a classroom routine and retell the steps.

🔢 Math

Compare two groups of classroom objects using more, fewer, or same.

🔬 Inquiry

Identify cause-and-effect relationships between actions, routines, and classroom outcomes.

💛 SEL

Practice cooperation, turn-taking, and a calm strategy for solving a small problem.

🎨 Workshop

Work in pairs to sequence a routine, then explain why it matters.

🧸 Centers

Routine cards, math comparison, building challenge, dramatic play, movement/music, book corner, calm spot.

📋 Teacher Evidence

Can the child explain a routine as a sequence and give a reason why it helps people learn or stay safe?

🧩 Put the Routine in Order

Tap the cards in the order you think makes sense.

Choose the first step.

Thursday · Apply — How Can We Help Our Learning Community?

Objective: Children apply what they have learned by creating a representation of the classroom and choosing a helpful responsibility.

🎵 Morning Circle

Review observations, sorting rules, and routines. Ask, “What can I do that helps someone else learn?”

📚 Literacy

Add labels, dictated language, or attempted writing to a classroom map or responsibility card.

🔢 Math

Count and place map features; compare quantities when deciding where materials belong.

🔬 Inquiry

Collaboratively create a simple classroom map using symbols for important spaces.

💛 SEL

Choose and rehearse one realistic classroom responsibility.

🎨 Workshop

Build the class map in teams. Negotiate where features belong and explain choices.

🧸 Centers

Map-making, name/label station, math materials, dramatic play, movement/music, book corner, calm spot.

📋 Teacher Evidence

Can the child contribute an idea, use a simple representation, and explain how their action helps the community?

🗺 Build a Tiny Classroom Map

Tap each square to cycle through classroom symbols.

🤝 Choose a Helpful Job

Friday · Demonstrate — I Am Part of the Learning Garden

Objective: Children demonstrate what they understand about belonging, routines, organization, counting, and helping others.

🎵 Morning Circle

Revisit the week's question and share one thing noticed, learned, or now possible.

📚 Literacy

Identify their name and respond to a short read-aloud question about community or belonging.

🔢 Math

Complete a hands-on count, match, sort, or comparison task.

🔬 Inquiry

Use the classroom map or routine sequence to explain how one part of the community works.

💛 SEL

Demonstrate a help-seeking or helping behavior.

🌟 Workshop

Complete the weekly mastery check, add one artifact to the portfolio, and celebrate growth.

🧸 Centers

Choice-based review centers with teacher conferencing and targeted observation.

📋 Teacher Evidence

Collect one literacy sample, one math demonstration, one inquiry explanation, and one SEL observation.

Choose one sentence that feels true today.
