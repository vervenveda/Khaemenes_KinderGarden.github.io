Monday: Wonder Launch

Daily learning target: Match three familiar tools to appropriate tasks and explain one safe-use rule, including when an adult's permission is needed.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can tools help us create safely?”

Literacy: Sequence words: first, next, last

Math: Coding patterns and directional language

Inquiry: Simple tools and digital citizenship

SEL: Responsible choices

Workshop: Lay out picture cards for pencil, magnifier, paper, blocks, and other familiar tools alongside task cards. Learners match each tool to a reasonable job and sort examples into “use with permission” and “ask a grown-up first.” Do not require internet access or a working device.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Tuesday: Guided Skill Lab

Daily learning target: Create and follow a three-step unplugged algorithm using first, next, and last, then check whether another person can follow it.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can tools help us create safely?”

Literacy: Sequence words: first, next, last

Math: Coding patterns and directional language

Inquiry: Simple tools and digital citizenship

SEL: Responsible choices

Workshop: Give learners three picture cards for a familiar routine such as making a drawing or tidying materials. Arrange them as first/next/last, then exchange the order and predict what would become confusing. Have a partner or adult follow the original algorithm.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Wednesday: Inquiry Workshop

Daily learning target: Guide a paper-token “robot” through a simple grid using directional words, find one unclear or incorrect instruction, and repair it.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can tools help us create safely?”

Literacy: Sequence words: first, next, last

Math: Coding patterns and directional language

Inquiry: Simple tools and digital citizenship

SEL: Responsible choices

Workshop: Build a paper grid and choose a start and finish square. Use arrow cards and words such as forward, turn, and stop to guide a paper token. Intentionally include one ambiguous or wrong step, ask the learner to test it, and help revise only the step that caused the mismatch.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Thursday: Create & Communicate

Daily learning target: Design a short set of tool-use or classroom instructions, test them with a partner or grown-up, and revise one step for clarity or safety.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can tools help us create safely?”

Literacy: Sequence words: first, next, last

Math: Coding patterns and directional language

Inquiry: Simple tools and digital citizenship

SEL: Responsible choices

Workshop: Learners create a three-to-five-step instruction strip for a familiar classroom task using pictures, symbols, marks, or words. A partner follows it literally; the author notices a missing or unclear step and revises the strip. Include a “pause and ask” safety step where appropriate.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Friday: Review, Assessment & Reflection

Daily learning target: Solve a new tool-and-sequence scenario, identify one digital privacy boundary, and explain a safe next step when unsure.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can tools help us create safely?”

Literacy: Sequence words: first, next, last

Math: Coding patterns and directional language

Inquiry: Simple tools and digital citizenship

SEL: Responsible choices

Workshop: Present an unfamiliar picture scenario involving a tool, a sequence, and private information. Ask what tool fits, what should happen first, and what details must not be shared. Record the explanation or chosen response and one learner-selected improvement.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.
