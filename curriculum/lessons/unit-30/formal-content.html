Unit 30

Art, Music, and Pattern Makers

Creative process, music, visual arts, dance, media, critique, and reflection

Essential Question

How can patterns become art and music?

Monday: Wonder Launch

Daily objective: Keep a steady four-beat pulse and distinguish it from a changing rhythm.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can patterns become art and music?”

Literacy: Describe art and music

Math: Patterns, shapes, and counting beats

Inquiry: Sound, vibration, and design

SEL: Creative courage

Original activity: Model four evenly spaced taps. Children echo with hands, gentle percussion, movements, or silent counters.

Practice check (not formal mastery): Represents four steady beats.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Tuesday: Guided Skill Lab

Daily objective: Create and explain a repeating AB or AAB pattern using colors and sounds.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can patterns become art and music?”

Literacy: Describe art and music

Math: Patterns, shapes, and counting beats

Inquiry: Sound, vibration, and design

SEL: Creative courage

Original activity: Arrange two original sound symbols, perform AB and AAB patterns, then predict the next sound.

Practice check (not formal mastery): Extends a pattern and names or points to its repeating unit.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Wednesday: Inquiry Workshop

Daily objective: Observe how changing tempo differs from changing loudness.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can patterns become art and music?”

Literacy: Describe art and music

Math: Patterns, shapes, and counting beats

Inquiry: Sound, vibration, and design

SEL: Creative courage

Original activity: Play a safe soft sound slowly and quickly; compare tempo, then gentle and stronger sounds to compare loudness.

Practice check (not formal mastery): Distinguishes a tempo change from a loudness change.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Thursday: Create & Communicate

Daily objective: Compose and revise a four-beat musical phrase using a picture score.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can patterns become art and music?”

Literacy: Describe art and music

Math: Patterns, shapes, and counting beats

Inquiry: Sound, vibration, and design

SEL: Creative courage

Original activity: Draw four sound symbols, perform them, change one symbol, and explain the revision.

Practice check (not formal mastery): Shares a four-beat phrase and indicates one revision.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Friday: Review, Assessment & Reflection

Daily objective: Identify a repeating unit in a new sound pattern and share a creative choice.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How can patterns become art and music?”

Literacy: Describe art and music

Math: Patterns, shapes, and counting beats

Inquiry: Sound, vibration, and design

SEL: Creative courage

Original activity: Host a voluntary composition sharing circle and give children a new pattern to extend.

Practice check (not formal mastery): Recognizes a new pattern and explains one choice.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.
