Monday: Wonder Launch

Daily learning target: Identify two familiar classroom or community symbols and explain one meaning.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How do symbols help people remember and belong?”

Literacy: Symbol labels and shared pledge of care

Math: Sort symbols by attributes

Inquiry: Civic symbols and classroom agreements

SEL: Respectful participation

Original guided activity: Examine teacher-drawn classroom and community symbols. Compare what can be seen with what each means; invite pointing, naming or drawing.

Practice evidence (not formal mastery): Child identifies two symbols and communicates one meaning.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Tuesday: Guided Skill Lab

Daily learning target: Sort six symbol cards by a chosen attribute and explain the grouping rule.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How do symbols help people remember and belong?”

Literacy: Symbol labels and shared pledge of care

Math: Sort symbols by attributes

Inquiry: Civic symbols and classroom agreements

SEL: Respectful participation

Original guided activity: Sort self-drawn symbol cards by shape, color or purpose. Change the rule and discuss why groups changed.

Practice evidence (not formal mastery): Child groups cards and indicates the sorting rule.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Wednesday: Inquiry Workshop

Daily learning target: Compare two symbols and explain how each helps people communicate.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How do symbols help people remember and belong?”

Literacy: Symbol labels and shared pledge of care

Math: Sort symbols by attributes

Inquiry: Civic symbols and classroom agreements

SEL: Respectful participation

Original guided activity: Examine educator-designed quiet-space and crossing symbols. Discuss users, meanings and different settings.

Practice evidence (not formal mastery): Child matches each symbol to its purpose.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Thursday: Create & Communicate

Daily learning target: Design an original belonging symbol with at least two purposeful features.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How do symbols help people remember and belong?”

Literacy: Symbol labels and shared pledge of care

Math: Sort symbols by attributes

Inquiry: Civic symbols and classroom agreements

SEL: Respectful participation

Original guided activity: Sketch and revise an original emblem using paper, shapes or tactile materials. Explain a meaningful color and shape.

Practice evidence (not formal mastery): Child creates a symbol and explains one deliberate choice.

Centers: Teacher table, Math/manipulative bin, Art/maker station, Dramatic play, Movement/music, Book corner, Calm reflection spot

Teacher evidence: Adult observation, child explanation, work sample, oral language, cooperative play, and assessment item.

Friday: Review, Assessment & Reflection

Daily learning target: Interpret a new contextual symbol and reflect on one respectful action.

Morning Circle: Welcome song, visual schedule, feelings check-in, and question of the day: “How do symbols help people remember and belong?”

Literacy: Symbol labels and shared pledge of care

Math: Sort symbols by attributes

Inquiry: Civic symbols and classroom agreements

SEL: Respectful participation

Original guided activity: Mix familiar and newly drawn signs. Use context clues to infer meaning and revisit misconceptions.

Practice evidence (not formal mastery): Child explains a symbol from clues and describes a respectful choice.
