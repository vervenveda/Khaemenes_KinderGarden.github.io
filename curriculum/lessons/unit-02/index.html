How this mentor works
  • NAIB is the friendly conversational layer for explanations, planning, guidance, and permitted mentor context.
  • NOEMA remains the administrative supervisor for permissions, memory, identity, guardian rules, and specialist boundaries.
  • The mentor may adapt examples, scaffolding, question style, and organization from expressed learning preferences and interests.
  • It does not infer intelligence from age or appearance, diagnose the child, or lower academic opportunity.
  • This static prototype uses bounded mentor actions rather than unrestricted child chat.
