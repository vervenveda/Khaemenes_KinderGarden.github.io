## Result

**PASS — source-level instructional alignment for Weeks 31–36.**

This certification supersedes the older structural warning that Weeks 31–36 lacked the exact phrase “independent practice.” Current source now contains a complete five-day depth cycle in every week. The audit evaluated the instructional function, not keyword presence.

## Daily depth evidence

Every one of the 30 daily lesson articles contains:

1. an observable daily learning target;
2. recall/prior-knowledge and vocabulary;
3. explicit adult modeling;
4. three guided examples;
5. a fresh independent transfer task;
6. corrective feedback;
7. teacher-observable/practice evidence;
8. accessible response or differentiation options.

Daily practice remains explicitly non-mastery evidence.

## Printable alignment

Canonical printable packets for Weeks 31–36 each provide five daily learner activities with Academy, learner, date, week, and Kindergarten identity. They remain practice artifacts and do not independently award formal mastery.

## Formal mastery alignment

`curriculum/data/a-plus-mastery.js` contains specialized ten-criterion rubrics for every Week 31–36. These replace the generic fallback for these weeks and align to the taught content.

- **31 — Bugs, Gardens, and Tiny Worlds:** observation vs inference, classification, counting, habitat, humane observation, field-guide evidence.
- **32 — Water Worlds:** habitat/resources, pond-river-ocean comparison, sorting, graphing, model limits, water safety.
- **33 — Sky Watchers:** sky observation, day/night sequence, Sun/Moon distinction, patterns, model limits, observation vs story, Sun safety.
- **34 — Technology and Coding:** tool purpose, algorithms, debugging, cause/effect, privacy, permission, accessibility, revision.
- **35 — Kindergarten Capstone:** question formation, evidence planning, literacy/math tool use, inquiry, model/maker evidence, revision, source/safety awareness.
- **36 — Kindergarten Review and Celebration:** integrated literacy, story sequence, emergent communication, counting, number composition, spatial/pattern/measurement application, inquiry, revision, portfolio growth, first-grade readiness.

Required-core criteria remain enforced by the existing mastery engine together with the 80% threshold and authorized-adult affirmation.
