Kindergarten Guardian Release

A child who already has a Pre-K account must keep the same learner ID when entering Kindergarten. A parent or authorized guardian reviews these conditions separately before formal Kindergarten lessons become available.

1 · Select existing childUse the Family Portal. Change the child's grade to Kindergarten (k); do not register the child again.
2 · Review & authorizeThe authorized adult confirms each of the seven Kindergarten acknowledgments below.
3 · Enter formal lessonsAfter successful local authorization, resume the child's first available Kindergarten lesson.
